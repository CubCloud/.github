# CubCloud AI

**Sovereign AI infrastructure and product development. Built in Montana.**

We own our stack — GPU to interface. H200s, H100s, and RTX PRO 6000s sitting in a data center in Missoula, not rented from a hyperscaler. When we say your data stays private, we mean it literally.

---

## What we build

**Infrastructure** — Bare-metal GPU compute available to businesses, researchers, and communities that need AI without handing their data to a cloud oligopoly. Montana-hosted, Montana-operated.

**Products** — AI tools built on our own rails:
- **CubDen** — Private, sovereign AI workspace for teams. Think your own internal AI portal, not a SaaS subscription to someone else's model.
- **CubCall** — AI-powered call handling.
- **CubAlpha** — Early access AI experiments. Things we're not ready to name yet.
- **CubLabs** — Where weird ideas go to find out if they're actually good ideas.
- **Marijuan.AI** and **Connected** — Vertical-specific AI tools. The names are self-explanatory on one of those.
- **Other Products** — We also build websites, backends, frontends, middle ends, AI agents, and the works for local companies. We'd share more, but NDAs are a thing. Just know that it's some of the best software ever written.

**Community** — CubCloud Community (501c3) and the MontanAI Alliance (501c6) exist to make sure AI literacy, tribal partnerships, healthcare, and academic enablement aren't afterthoughts in Montana's AI future. We're not waiting for someone else to do this.
